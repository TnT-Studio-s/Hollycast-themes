## Illustrations

The original lodge, portrait lodge, maple leaf and storybook cover were created
for this theme with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-06.
The landscape composition was refined to reserve room for native controls.
The portrait uses the original landscape as a visual reference. The leaf and
storybook cover were generated independently. Exact prompts are included in
`source/imagegen-prompts.md`.

Generated PNG files are distributed without resampling or recoloring. The
maple leaf retains its generated alpha channel. No third-party game media is
included, and the theme does not load remote resources.
