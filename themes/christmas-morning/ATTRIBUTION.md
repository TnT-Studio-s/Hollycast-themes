- `assets/christmas-room.png`, `christmas-room-portrait.png`,
  `christmas-title.png` and `hollycast-christmas.png` were generated for this
  theme using OpenAI image generation. Their PNG pixels and transparency are
  retained unchanged. The prompts and reference roles are documented in
  [source/imagegen-prompts.md](source/imagegen-prompts.md).
- `assets/crt-glass.png` and `crt-retune.png` are original procedural textures
  generated by [source/generate_crt.py](source/generate_crt.py).
- `assets/ChristmasSerif-Regular.ttf` and `ChristmasSerif-Bold.ttf` are modified
  Libre Baskerville fonts renamed **Hollycast Christmas Serif**. The original
  font and derivatives use the SIL Open Font License 1.1, not the theme's GPL.
  Full upstream copyright and reserved-name notices are retained in
  [assets/OFL-LibreBaskerville.txt](assets/OFL-LibreBaskerville.txt). Sources,
  modifications and rebuilding are documented in
  [source/FONTS.md](source/FONTS.md).

The catalogue images are real Hollycast captures. Game covers and gameplay
preview imagery shown there belong to their respective owners; they are not
original theme artwork and are not included in the theme ZIP. No ROMs, game
