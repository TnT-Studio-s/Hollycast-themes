# Signal Bloom background prompts

## Landscape

Production background for the Signal Bloom Hollycast library theme, landscape
16:9. Full-bleed midnight navy and near-black indigo, extremely subtle horizontal
CRT scanlines, sparse cyan and coral pinpoints, fine electric-cyan and warm-coral
arcs sweeping in from the upper right and lower corners, with a faint technical
grid near the edges. Keep the center from x=5% to 95% and y=13% to 89% dark,
quiet and low-detail for real library tiles and text. Premium late-1990s console
interface atmosphere. No text, logos, icons, game artwork, screenshots,
characters, objects, UI, buttons, frames or watermark.

## Portrait

Production background matching the Signal Bloom landscape, portrait 2:3. Full
bleed midnight indigo and near-black, very subtle horizontal CRT scanlines,
sparse cyan and coral pinpoints, fine orbital arcs along the far sides and
corners, and technical details only at the edges. Keep the middle 80% quiet and
dark for a real library list and metadata. No text, logos, game imagery,
screenshots, characters, objects, UI controls, frames or watermark.
